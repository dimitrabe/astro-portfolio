These pieces themselves form a collage, since they have been placed next to one another like a puzzle. On the porcelain fragments — inspired by the portrait photograph — parts of the face are reflected, expressing different emotions that clash with one another: joy, anger, sadness, contemplation. All these pieces and all these different emotions coexist anything but harmoniously during creative expression, and it is quite likely that the image of the person does not differ much from the digital composition during the act of creation, with every possible emotion flooding through them. But it is precisely this abundance and this conflict, if expressed, that form the core of creation and liberate the creative individual, allowing them to coexist with themselves. The result of this creative release is the flowers which, despite the adverse conditions — both literally in the urban landscape and within the mind and psyche of the artist — find a way to bloom in the cracks.
