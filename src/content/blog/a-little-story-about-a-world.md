In October 2018, a small star was torn apart and devoured when it drifted dangerously close to a black hole in a galaxy 665 million light years from Earth. This violent event is seen quite often in the night sky.
Three years later and without having consumed another star, the black hole “burped”, ejecting remnants of stellar matter (Whitehead, 2023).
This event inspired a short story about loneliness, relationships, the violence of love, limits, the possibility of choice or lack thereof.
Whitehead, N. (2023, November 9). Black Hole burps up shredded star years after consuming it. Harvard Gazette. https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2022/10/black-hole-burps-up-shredded-star-years-after-consuming-it/
